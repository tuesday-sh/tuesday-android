Tuesday.sh is built on real IT Pro standards and best practices tuned for home networks. Track every network device for vulnerability notices, recalls, and zero-day exploits. A calm, rapid-triage tool and Patch Tuesday readiness companion for everyday networks and homelab builders.

Build an accurate inventory of the gear plugged into your home—from Wi-Fi routers and mesh access points to NAS boxes, cameras, smart TVs, and printers—and check them on a calm monthly cadence.

KEY FEATURES
• Private, Local-First Inventory: Your device list, custom names, room locations, and check-in history stay right on your Android device in portable plain-text JSON files. Export or import backups anytime—no account or cloud login required.
• Fast Offline Hardware Lookup: Search thousands of home and homelab devices by brand, model, retail UPC barcode, MAC address prefix, or FCC ID right on your phone.
• Live Barcode, QR & Photo Discovery: Scan UPC barcodes, MAC address stickers, or QR codes with your camera, or snap a photo of a tricky chassis label. Photos are processed in memory and immediately discarded—we never store your pictures.
• Action-First Advisories & End-of-Life Guard: Matches CISA KEV notices (the U.S. government's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list—bugs actively targeted in the wild), physical safety recalls, vendor End-of-Life (EOL—when a manufacturer stops shipping security updates), and firmware releases directly to your gear.
• Hardware Lifespan Timeline: See vendor support windows at a glance, spot aging gear that's past its support cutoff, and log quick seasonal check-ins.
• Calm Check-Up Rhythm & Report Exports: Stay on top of new gear, monthly Patch Tuesday reviews, quarterly safety checks, and annual hardware audits without noisy alerts.
